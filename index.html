Research

My primary research interest is Reinforcement Learning, particularly applied RL in Data Science. I am also interested in applied Machine Learning and Deep Learning.

I have a particular fascination with Graph Theory— its structures, abstractions, and problem-solving perspectives genuinely intrigue me, and I enjoy exploring and brainstorming graph-based problems beyond my formal academic work.

Projects

Comparative analysis of Microarray, Bulk RNA-seq, and Single-Cell RNA-seq datasets using bioinformatics and machine learning pipelines.

Detection of Disease-Related Genetic Variants using BWT and Machine Learning

A probabilistic machine learning project involving sequence alignment, variant detection, sparse learning, and graph-based gene interaction modeling.

A scalable data science project using PySpark, graph analytics, Node2Vec embeddings, clustering, and machine learning.
